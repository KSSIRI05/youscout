# Project brief

## Source

Based on `Projet ALD (25-26) (1).pdf`, INPT, Architectures Logicielles Distribuées - ASEDS, Prof. Driss ALLAKI, academic year 2025/2026, pages 1-3. The PDF is the assignment source; statements marked proposed are team planning recommendations rather than assignment requirements.

## Purpose

YouScout gives football talent visibility, connects fans with players, coaches and scouts, and offers recruiters a worldwide community of potential talent. Users publish football skill videos through a mobile application inspired by the short-video social network format.

## Audience and growth

The brief anticipates thousands of users at launch and potentially millions after marketing succeeds. Initial markets are Morocco, England, France, Spain, Portugal, Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium and Italy. Later phases target MENA, sub-Saharan Africa, Latin America and eventually the world. Budget is not constrained as a strategic orientation.

## Assignment deliverables

1. Identify explicit and implicit architecture characteristics and prioritize a short list.
2. Compare architecture styles and justify the selected style.
3. Draw the topology, identify components, and document architectural decisions as ADRs.
4. Implement the architecture foundation with AI assistance, prioritizing a critical MVP.
5. Build a cross-platform mobile frontend with AI assistance.
6. Deploy to a suitable environment and justify the choice.

Architecture design is worth 14 points; implementation and deployment are worth 6. Work is performed in groups of four. Evaluation includes a presentation and demonstration. Final delivery is a GitHub link with complete source, ADRs and launch instructions.

## Open product questions

- Rating scale and permitted skill evaluations.
- Feed ranking and discoverability rules.
- Video duration, size and retention limits.
- Account closure and content retention behavior.
- Blocking behavior across feeds, profiles and chat.
- Dashboard metrics and moderation appeal workflow.
- Languages, age policy, and scout verification.

These details are not specified by the brief and must be resolved before implementation.
